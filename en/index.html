The problem

Knowing you are late is easy. Deciding what to cut is not.

Shorten the shower, or skip breakfast? While you do the math, another five minutes are gone. TIVER makes that call for you, using rules you set once when you were calm.

The app interface is in Korean for now. Weather and place search use Korean services, so outside Korea the weather falls back to Seoul.

How it works

Four words are all you need

Daily Stream

Your day flows sideways like a river. The green NOW line marks this moment, so the time left is something you see instead of calculate.

Time Drop

One thing you do, with a start time and a length. Change one and the ones after it move by themselves.

Anchor

A moment that cannot move — leaving for work, a class, a meeting. TIVER counts backwards from it and tells you when to head out.

Magic Zone

The stretch before an anchor. Mark each time drop as must do, can shorten, or can skip. On a late morning, one tap folds the stretch to fit.

Magic Zone

What one tap actually does

  1. Drops you already missed and marked “can skip” are dropped.
  2. “Must do” stays as it is.
  3. If that is not enough, “can shorten” shrinks — down to half.
  4. Still short? The earliest remaining “can skip” drops go next.
  5. As a last resort, “must do” shrinks, but never below 90%.
  6. If it still does not fit, TIVER tells you how many minutes late you may be. It does not block you.
